This is the pre release demo for Squatch Quest, the full digital version can be found here: https://seasidestudios.itch.io/squatch-quest

Story:

Sammy Squatchman just wants to go surfing with his raccoon buddies but the Dark Warlock has taken over the forest with his evil minions. Sammy must search the forest to find the missing emeralds of the Forest Temple and restore sasquatch magic to Takwahna Village, only then can the Dark Warlock be defeated.

About:

The map is based on a real location, which may or may not be inhabited by sasquatches. The full game reveals several clues, donate to help fund this game and finally find out!

Game Play:

Squatch Quest is an adventure style game, move from screen to screen fighting minions and solving puzzles; similar to The Legend of Zelda.
