spruce's per-zone LED color and brightness settings for the TrimUI Brick and
Brick Pro were inspired by these two apps, which showed it could be done.
spruce ships no code from either. Its LED settings are its own, built on
TrimUI's /sys/class/led_anim driver and a zone map measured on the devices.
